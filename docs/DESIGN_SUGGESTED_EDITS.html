Suggested Edits: docs/DESIGN.md

These are suggestions only - nothing in docs/DESIGN.md has been changed. The goal is reducing it to the 1-2 page design brief the original assessment brief calls for, covering: how the problem was understood, proposed architecture, key tradeoffs and assumptions, how rate limits/durability were handled, and what was intentionally not built.

Top-line issue

docs/DESIGN.md is currently ~2,700 words (5-6 pages). It reads as an engineering working-doc - full model field lists, phase-by-phase tracking, Docker Compose wiring specifics - rather than the five-part brief the assessment asks for. Getting to 1-2 pages means cutting content that already lives in the code, migrations, and docs/CLAUDE_IMPLEMENTATION.html, not just trimming sentences.

Suggested structure

Rename sections to match the assessment's five bullets exactly, so a reviewer skimming for grading criteria finds each one immediately:

  1. How I understood the problem - keep the opening two paragraphs, tighten to ~3-4 sentences.
  2. Proposed architecture - condensed version of "Pull Service Object" + "Continuous Ingestion" + "Fan Out."
  3. Key tradeoffs and assumptions - pull together "Why persist to Postgres," "Known gap," the _unenrichable-per-event tradeoff, and the fixed-vs-priority-budget decision.
  4. How I handled rate limits and durability - condensed "Rate limits" section.
  5. What I intentionally did not build - keep almost as-is, it's already brief-appropriate.
